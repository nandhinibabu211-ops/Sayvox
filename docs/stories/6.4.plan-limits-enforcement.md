# Story 6.4: Plan limits enforcement

## Status
Draft

## Story
**As the** business,
**we want** every plan limit enforced consistently,
**so that** pricing reflects cost and paid plans deliver what they promise.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Monthly/daily minutes, presets and dictionary size are enforced per plan; caches are invalidated on plan change.

## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Central `getPlanLimits(userId)` used by limits, settings and dictionary modules (AC 1)
- [ ] On downgrade: preset outside plan → reset to `balanced`; dictionary over limit → keep terms but stop sending extras as keyterms, show notice (AC 1)
- [ ] Integration tests for upgrade and downgrade transitions (AC 1)
