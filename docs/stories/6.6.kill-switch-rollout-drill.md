# Story 6.6: Kill switch and staged rollout drill

## Status
Draft

## Story
**As the** operator,
**I want** to rehearse pausing the service and rolling back a release,
**so that** we can act fast during an incident.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. Kill switch toggled in staging and a prod canary; clients pause within 30 min (or on the next press, via `/session/start` enforcement).
2. Rollback of a bad client release is rehearsed.

## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Runbook `docs/runbooks/kill-switch.md` (who, how, comms template) (AC 1)
- [ ] Drill: enable kill switch → verify next press is blocked with message → disable (AC 1)
- [ ] Drill: ship a canary "bad" build to 10 % in staging → roll back manifest → verify clients return to good version (AC 2)
- [ ] Record drill results and timings (AC 1, 2)
