# Story 5.6: Privacy page, terms and in-app disclosure

## Status
Draft

## Story
**As a** user,
**I want** to know exactly where my voice and text go,
**so that** I can trust Sayvox with sensitive dictation.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. `apps/web` privacy page: audio → ElevenLabs, text → the selected model provider via OpenRouter, hosting on Google Cloud, nothing stored by Sayvox.
2. Terms of service page; consent captured at sign-up (DPDP/GDPR).
3. OpenRouter routing restricted to providers that don't train on or keep prompts; ElevenLabs zero-retention plan status documented.

## Tasks / Subtasks
- [ ] Draft privacy page (data flows diagram, processors list, retention, rights, contact) and terms; send for legal review (AC 1, 2)
- [ ] Mention clipboard managers may capture transient text (Story 1.7 note) (AC 1)
- [ ] Consent checkbox + timestamp stored on `users` (add `consented_at`, `terms_version` columns) (AC 2)
- [ ] In-app Privacy & Account screen with same plain-language summary (AC 1)
- [ ] Verify OpenRouter's no-training/no-retention routing field (e.g. `provider.data_collection: deny`) is sent on every request; ask ElevenLabs about zero-retention; record in `research.md` (AC 3)
- [ ] DPAs in place with ElevenLabs, Google Cloud, Razorpay and OpenRouter (AC 2)

## Dev Notes
- Compliance: DPDP Act (India), GDPR — architecture §15, brief §10. This is not legal advice; legal sign-off required before public launch.
