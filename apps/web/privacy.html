Privacy

Sayvox turns your voice into text at your cursor. Your words pass through two outside services so that can happen. Sayvox itself never stores what you say or what gets written.

Where your words go

  1. Your PC. Sayvox records the microphone only while you hold the hotkey. Audio is never saved to disk.
  2. Audio → ElevenLabs (speech recognition). Audio streams straight from your PC to ElevenLabs, which sends back the text. Sayvox's servers never receive your audio. ElevenLabs offers zero retention only on enterprise plans, which Sayvox does not have yet, so ElevenLabs may keep audio and transcripts under its own terms. We'll update this page when that changes.
  3. Text → an AI model through OpenRouter (Prompt and Email modes only). The transcript, and any text you chose to share as reply context, pass through Sayvox's server to OpenRouter and on to the model provider for your preset (for example Anthropic, OpenAI or Google). Every request is limited to providers that don't train on your data and don't keep prompts (OpenRouter data_collection: deny and zero data retention). Dictation mode never uses an AI model.
  4. Back to your cursor. The result is pasted through the Windows clipboard, then your previous clipboard is put back. Clipboard history tools (such as Windows' Win + V) may capture that text briefly.

What Sayvox stores

What Why Kept until
Email address and sign-in account To sign you in You delete your account
Settings and dictionary terms you add To work the way you set it up You change them or delete your account
Per-dictation numbers: seconds of audio, mode, app name (like slack.exe, never window titles), model used, timings, tokens Usage limits, billing and keeping the service fast You delete your account
Crash reports: error type, app version, code locations Fixing bugs. Error messages and text are removed before sending 30 days
Plan and payment status (payments handled by Razorpay) Billing Your account is deleted; Razorpay keeps invoices as tax law requires

Never stored by Sayvox: audio, transcripts, AI rewrites, the text you select for email replies, window titles.

Services we use

  • ElevenLabs: speech recognition.
  • OpenRouter and the model provider it routes to: AI rewrites.
  • Google Cloud: hosting, database, sign-in (Identity Platform), logging and crash reports.
  • Razorpay: payments for paid plans.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Privacy & Account. It removes your account, settings, dictionary and usage records, and signs you out.
  • Reply context is off by default. When you turn it on, only the text you've selected is read, once, for that reply.
  • Under India's DPDP Act and the EU GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Contact details will be added here before launch.
