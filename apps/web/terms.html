Terms of service

These terms cover your use of the Sayvox app for Windows and the Sayvox service. By creating an account you agree to them and to the privacy notice.

The service

Sayvox turns speech into text and, in Prompt and Email modes, rewrites it with an AI model. Transcription and rewrites can be wrong. Read what Sayvox writes before you send it, especially in email and chat. "Review before insert" in Settings → General lets you approve each AI rewrite first.

Your account

  • You need an account to use Sayvox. Keep your sign-in details to yourself.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the app.
  • We may suspend accounts that abuse the service, for example by automating requests or trying to get around usage limits.

Plans and limits

  • Each plan has daily and monthly dictation minutes, a dictionary size and a set of presets. When you reach a limit, dictation pauses until it resets or you upgrade.
  • Paid plans renew automatically through Razorpay until you cancel. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period stays active.

Your content

What you dictate and what Sayvox writes for you is yours. Sayvox doesn't store it; see the privacy notice for what passes through our providers. Don't use Sayvox for anything unlawful or to dictate content you have no right to share.

Updates and availability

The app updates itself. Very old versions may be asked to update before they keep working, and we may pause the service briefly to fix a problem. We aim for the service to be available but don't guarantee it.
