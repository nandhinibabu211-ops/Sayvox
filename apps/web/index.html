How it works

  1. Hold Ctrl + Shift in any app: email, Slack, Word, your code editor, a browser.
  2. Speak. You see the words as you go.
  3. Let go. The text appears at your cursor, and your clipboard is left as it was.

Three modes, picked for you

Dictation

"Um, let's meet Thursday, no wait, Friday" becomes "Let's meet Friday." Fillers and false starts are gone.

Prompt

In ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor, a rambling request turns into a clear, structured prompt.

Email & Slack

Say what you mean; get a tidy message in the right tone for the app.

Sayvox chooses the mode from the app you're in. You can change it per app, or tap the badge while you talk.
